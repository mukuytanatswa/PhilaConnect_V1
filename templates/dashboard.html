PhilaConnect Reminder Schedule
3-touch system · non-intrusive
📋
48 Hours Before
Appointment details + easy reschedule link. Sent at booking time or 10am if already within window.
🌅
Morning Of
Friendly same-day reminder at 7:00 AM with doctor name, time, and what to bring.
⏱
1 Hour Before
Final nudge. Includes clinic address and parking/transport tip. Option to confirm arrival.
Why 3 messages? Research shows 4+ reminders increase opt-outs. The 48hr message captures those who need to rearrange work/transport; the morning-of covers sleep-forgetting; the 1hr message prevents no-shows while there's still time to act. No redundant "day before evening" message — covered by morning-of instead.
